Collector Families

Hydroxamate

Chelating collector widely used for oxide minerals.

  • Charge: Anionic
  • HSAB: Hard base
  • Typical pH: 7-10
  • Mechanism: Chelation
  • Industrial use: Bauxite and iron ores

Fatty Acid

Anionic surfactant used in oxide and non-sulfide flotation.

  • Charge: Anionic
  • HSAB: Hard base
  • Typical pH: 8-11
  • Mechanism: Chemisorption
  • Industrial use: Oxide minerals

Amine

Cationic collector commonly used for silicate flotation.

  • Charge: Cationic
  • HSAB: Lewis base
  • Typical pH: 6-10
  • Mechanism: Electrostatic attraction
  • Industrial use: Quartz and clay minerals

Xanthate

The classical collector for sulfide flotation.

  • Charge: Anionic
  • HSAB: Soft base
  • Typical pH: 7-11
  • Mechanism: Chemisorption
  • Industrial use: Sulfide ores

Sulfonate

Anionic collector for selected industrial applications.

  • Charge: Anionic
  • HSAB: Hard base
  • Typical pH: 6-10
  • Mechanism: Surface complexation
  • Industrial use: Oxides and silicates
